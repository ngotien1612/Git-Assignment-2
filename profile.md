#US-103: User Profile Managemnent

##Update profile form
-Fields: full name, email, phone number, date of birth.
-Button: "Save changes" and "Cancel".

## Change avatar
-The user can upload a new avatar image from their device.

## Validation
- Email must have a valid format.
- Avatar must be JPG or PNG and not larger than 2MB.
- Show an error message when the input is invalid.

## Usage guide
1. Open the Profile page from the user menu.
2. Edit your information or click "Change avatar".
3. Click "Save changes" to update your personal details.
